Satyam is 4. His sister Tanya is 6. They should be fighting over toys, teasing each other, and getting scolded by their parents for making a mess. Instead, Satyam is fighting relapsed blood cancer. He beat it once already — but the cancer has returned, and this time chemotherapy alone won't be enough. Doctors say his best chance now is a bone marrow transplant, and in an extraordinary stroke of luck, his own sister Tanya is a match. The transplant, hospital stay and post-transplant care are estimated to cost upwards of ₹8,00,000 — money this family does not have. Every contribution, however small, moves Satyam closer to the transplant that could let him and Tanya go back to being ordinary siblings again.
